# About

## Project Identity

Mobilitetsmodellen is the fifth module of a twenty-project research-software portfolio on
income, wealth, taxation, inequality, and intergenerational mobility in Sweden, produced at
the Department of Economics, Stockholm University.

## Research Context

The repository estimates intergenerational income and rank mobility in Sweden by linking the
multi-generational register (Flergenerationsregistret) to the LISA panel and tax registers.
It addresses six formal research questions spanning absolute mobility, geographic heterogeneity,
DoubleML efficiency gains, mediation channels, shrinkage robustness, and cross-country
comparisons.
