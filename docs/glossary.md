# Glossary

**A-region**
SCB labour-market region; 75 functional regions defined by SCB for labour-market analysis.

**causal forest**
Heterogeneous-treatment-effect forest estimator (Wager and Athey, 2018; Athey et al., 2019)
with honest sample splitting and cross-fitting.

**DoubleML**
Double or debiased machine learning (Chernozhukov et al., 2018); an orthogonal-score
estimator for causal parameters in partially linear regression models.

**empirical Bayes**
Shrinkage estimator that uses the marginal distribution of data to estimate a prior, then
forms posterior estimates for each group. Reduces mean-squared error relative to raw estimates.

**Equal Opportunity Project**
Research programme led by Raj Chetty and colleagues producing county-level and neighbourhood-
level intergenerational mobility estimates for the United States.

**FA-region**
Functional analytical region (funktionell analysregion); 60 regions used by Tillvaxtanalys
to delineate Swedish labour-market areas.

**Flergenerationsregistret**
Swedish multi-generational register maintained by SCB; links individuals to biological and
adoptive parents across at least two generations.

**IGE**
Intergenerational income elasticity; the slope in a log-log regression of child lifetime
income on parent lifetime income.

**IFS UK**
Institute for Fiscal Studies; provider of UK intergenerational mobility benchmarks.

**James-Stein**
Classical empirical-Bayes shrinkage estimator (James and Stein, 1961) that dominates the
sample mean in mean-squared error when there are three or more groups.

**kommun**
Swedish municipality; 290 kommuner as of 2024.

**lan**
Swedish county (laen); 21 laen as of 2024.

**mediation analysis**
Decomposition of a total causal effect into a direct effect and an indirect effect running
through a specified mediator variable.

**Moran's I**
Spatial-autocorrelation statistic measuring the correlation between a variable at each
location and its spatial neighbours.

**OECD IDD**
OECD Income Distribution Database; harmonised cross-country data on income distribution
and poverty.

**rank-rank slope**
Regression coefficient in an OLS regression of child income rank on parent income rank
within birth cohort; the primary measure of relative intergenerational mobility.

**spatial Bayes**
Empirical-Bayes shrinkage with a spatially correlated prior modelled as a Gaussian-Markov
random field over the municipality network.

**transition matrix**
Quintile-to-quintile matrix of conditional probabilities; entry (i, j) is the probability
that a child born to a parent in quintile i ends up in quintile j.
